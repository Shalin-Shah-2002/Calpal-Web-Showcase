CalPal Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CalPal - Calorie Tracker AI (package: com.shalinshah.calpal) collects, uses, stores, and deletes user data.

Last updated: 27 March 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Profile information such as name, age range, height, weight, and fitness preferences.
  • Meal logs, nutrition goals, progress inputs, and activity entries you provide.
  • Technical and diagnostics data such as app version, device OS, and crash logs.
  • Support information you provide when contacting us.

2. How We Use Data

  • To provide calorie tracking, nutrition insights, and account features.
  • To improve app quality, reliability, and fraud/abuse prevention.
  • To communicate account, product, and policy updates.

3. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Limited data may be processed by service providers for hosting, analytics, diagnostics, and AI functionality under contractual safeguards.
  • Data may be disclosed if required by law or valid legal process.

4. Data Retention

  • Account and nutrition log data is retained while your account remains active.
  • If your account is inactive for 24 months, we may delete or permanently de-identify account data from active systems.
  • Support request records are generally retained for up to 12 months.
  • Encrypted backups are rotated and generally deleted within 35 days.

5. How To Request Data Deletion

  • Email 2002shalin@gmail.com from your registered account email with subject: Delete My CalPal Data.
  • Include your account email and display name so we can locate your account.
  • We verify deletion requests before processing to protect account security.
  • After verification, we delete or permanently de-identify account data from active systems within 30 days in most cases.
  • Some limited records may be retained longer when required by law, security, tax, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution obligations.

6. Contact

Privacy questions, access requests, and deletion requests: 2002shalin@gmail.com
